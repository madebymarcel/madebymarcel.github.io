About and rules

Two teams of five, two 10-minute halves. The referee checks goals, possession, the shot clock, and restarts. Tackles, paddle fouls, and cards aren't modeled.

Training runs locally. Only completed generations are saved when you close the page. This version imports v3 snapshots.
